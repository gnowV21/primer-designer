## Prediction method
- Base NN: SantaLucia 1998 (DOI 10.1073/pnas.95.4.1460) via primer3-py.
- Salt correction: `salt_corrections_method='santalucia'` (primer3-py default). Owczarzy 2008 (Mg²⁺) and Owczarzy 2004 (Na⁺) are **independent** validation datasets — no circularity.
- Validation datasets are independent of the SantaLucia 1998 training set.
- Short oligos (≤12 nt): larger prediction errors are expected (boundary of NN validity).
- Experimental noise floor: σ(Tm) ≈ 0.3 °C (Owczarzy 2008, UV-melting). Model errors within 1–2 °C are excellent relative to this baseline.
- ⚠ DNA:RNA hybrid: RT primer Tm uses DNA:DNA NN params. Actual RT primer Tm on miRNA target (DNA:RNA hybrid) may differ by 3–8 °C (Sugimoto 1995 DNA:RNA params not implemented).
