## 4. Caveats

- hsa-miR-16-5p is matched against Chen, Bosmajian & Woo 2024 (*Biol Methods Protoc*, PMID 38783988); the lab's 2020 Vietnamese conference paper is cited as a second, non-PubMed-indexed source.
- hsa-miR-155-5p: the published RT overhangs (Czimmerer 2013, Yang 2014) target the older 23-nt annotation; miRBase v22 gives 24 nt, so only the forward core is scored — see the note in reference_mirnas.json.
- Chen 2005 (the original stem-loop paper): 6 RT primers are byte-identical to the tool. For miR-7, miR-10b, miR-20a, let-7d and let-7e the paper used the 2005 mature sequences, whose 3' ends miRBase has since changed; let-7a used a 5-nt overhang; let-7b's listed overhang is not complementary to let-7b (source typo). Those RT primers are not scored; their forward cores are.
- Plant miRNAs (ath-*, Varkonyi-Gasic 2007) validate primer design only: the family/overhang-collision index covers human/mouse/rat and BLAST covers human cDNA, so the tool reports 'no family data — cannot assess' for them, which is not evidence of specificity.
- EBV (Cosmopoulos 2009): 30 of the paper's 40 assays are included — those whose primers are complementary to a Table I sequence. Not scored: BHRF1-2, BHRF1-3, BART1-3p, BART12 (RT overhang binds 1 nt past the listed 3' end, i.e. a +1 isoform), BART1-5p, BART8*, BART9 (binds 1 nt short, a -1 isoform), BART10 (forward skips the first nt), the 'BART3' row (forward matches neither Table I nor miRBase v22), BART14 (paper: no primer). Rows 'BART3*' and 'BART14*' carry the primers of BART3 and BART14 (label mix-up) and are scored under the sequence they match.
- 4 entries (miR-125b-5p, miR-9-3p, miR-221-3p, miR-222-3p) cite **unpublished lab-internal bachelor theses** — recorded as such, not presented as peer-reviewed sources.
- 2026-10 additions (46 assays, `benchmark_group` set): RT 'BODY PASS (n-nt overhang)' = Chen body identical, overhang length differs (Zhang 2007 prints 5 nt, its own MGB probe implies 8; Zheng 2014 uses 8). Bagheri 2021 uses a MODIFIED Chen stem (CGAGAG/CACTCG) — body not scored, 7-nt overhang and forward scored; its forwards have no 5' tail and start at miRNA nt 2-3; its 'miR-103' is hsa-miR-103a-2-5p. Zheng 2014's printed reverse belongs to another stem-loop body. Wang 2018 let-7c/let-7i use the Chen body and match byte-for-byte. Cao 2023: the printed RT overhangs of mmu-miR-467a-5p and mmu-miR-34c-5p are misprints (contradicted by the paper's own probes) — not scored; 5 forwards with a 9-nt miRNA core (< 10) are recorded, not scored. Mou 2013: the tool designs osa-miR1846a-5p and osa-miR1858a, which the paper's stem-loop assay failed to detect experimentally (low abundance, GC-rich) — design success is not detection.
- The forward specific-region check is weak evidence: when both primers anchor the miRNA 5' end, their cores agree by construction. It confirms anchoring and core length >= 10 nt, not primer equivalence.
- This report is distinct from, and does not use, the miR-16 "exp 220826" wet-lab case study in AUDIT_REPORT.md §3, which is explicitly not ground truth.
- The 50-miRNA panel in `benchmark/panel.json` has no external tool configured (`MIRPRIMER_TOOL` unset) — those rows in primer_benchmark_export.csv show this tool's own output only, no published-primer columns.
